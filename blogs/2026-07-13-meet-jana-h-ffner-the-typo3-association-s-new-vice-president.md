This year's TYPO3 board elections brought new leadership to the table, including Jana Höffner as Vice President. Jana isn't an agency owner, nor a developer, but someone who works diligently in the TYPO3 backend on a daily basis. She saw the need for an editor's point of view and acknowledged how this user group often gets overlooked in the grand scheme of things.
