Proposed bylaw amendments introduce Units and a Unit Cooperation Panel to organize volunteer work on TYPO3 CMS, giving the product its own governance alongside the Board and the Business Control Committee. Online voting runs 21–29 October 2026, and the changes need a two-thirds majority.
